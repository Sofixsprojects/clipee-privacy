Privacy Policy

App: Clipee · Package: com.clipee.app · Effective date: November 22, 2026

Clipee is a video-clipping app that helps creators turn long videos into short-form clips. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information you provide

Clipee does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or other personal identifiers to use its core features.

Media and device access

To create clips, you choose a video from your device. Clipee accesses only the media file you select for previews, trimming, framing, and export. Your videos and exported clips stay on your device. Clipee does not upload, sync, scan, or access your full photo or video library.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect personal data through the app. We do not sell or share personal data with third parties. We do not use your media files for advertising, profiling, or analytics.

Permissions

The app requests only the media access needed to let you select and process videos you choose. If you deny a permission, the app may not be able to import or save videos.

Contact

Changes

We may update this policy if the app changes. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
